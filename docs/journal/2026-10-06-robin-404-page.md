# A 404 page with our buttons, and fewer em dashes

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Author:** Robin, with Claude Code
- **Pull request:** https://github.com/nudge-code/side-project-nudge/pull/18

**Why:** the 404 and error pages copied the button styles by hand, and the copy still had em dashes, a common sign of AI-written text.

**What changed:**
- The 404 page offers "Try the practice case" next to "Go home", and its browser tab says "Page not found | Nudge".
- The 404 and error pages use our `Button` component instead of hand-written styles.
- The hero, the FAQ intro and the `/nudge-next` title no longer use em dashes.

**Files:** `apps/web/src/app/not-found.tsx`, `apps/web/src/app/error.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/organisms/hero-section.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/organisms/faq-section.tsx`, `apps/web/src/app/nudge-next/page.tsx`, `docs/roadmap.md`

**Checked:** lint, typecheck and build pass on Node 22; `/nope` answers 404 with both buttons; no em dash left on the home page.
