# Nudge / Le déclic

## Project and scope
An isolated desktop exploration at `/nudge-next` for people aged roughly 20–30 learning investing through fictional decisions. The review should establish a desirable brand world and an understandable learning interaction. The production home and practice journey remain intact. Phase 2 only; no full brand system, rollout or deployment.

## Directional axes
- Conversational: precise explanations without classroom language.
- Controlled maximalist: a concentrated purple brand canvas, quieter working surfaces.
- Peer: explore trade-offs, never prescribe an investment.
- Resonant: an abstract investing concept becomes something a person can manipulate.

## Synthesis
A folded N is the moment a reaction becomes understanding. Broad strips assemble into the mark, divide into exposures and become progress notches. Space Grotesk provides a compact, geometric display voice; DM Sans carries longer explanations. Violet #6236E8, ink #231B32, warm white #FAF9F6, lilac #D8CBFF and a restrained citron #E9F28A form the world. The logo has one geometry across navigation, the hero and small markers. Data uses direct labels and proportional lengths, not invented market curves.

## Composition
Navigation; an edge-dominant purple hero with large, staggered type and a folded N; a pale working canvas with case context, a proportional loss/contribution diagram and choice controls; an asymmetric topic explorer; a segmented, session-only learning trail. Hero motion runs once. Other motion answers actions. Reduced motion loses no content.

## Deliberate removals from the proposed exploration
The old case-led hero, student-only positioning, dark three-step strip, FAQ, waitlist form and repeated card containers are not repeated on this route. No changes to their production implementations.

## Rejections
No currency sculptures, lifestyle photography, gradients, glass, random floating, recommendations, savings tools, fake historical data, generic KPI grid or full-course dashboard.

## Implementation decisions
Original SVG geometry is preferable to generated raster imagery for this concept: it scales, animates and carries exact proportions. All visible EN/FR copy lives outside components. Locale switches preserve React state. Completion reflects interaction in this session, never a fabricated history. Financial examples are explicitly fictional, fees and taxes excluded, no predicted recovery.

## Open questions for review
Does the folded N merit developing into the final identity? How expressive should future scenario art become? Integration into the production homepage awaits review.
