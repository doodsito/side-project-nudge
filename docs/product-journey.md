# Nudge prelaunch learning journey

## Who it is for

Students nearing graduation and people starting their first job who want to understand saving and investing through practical choices. The site teaches reasoning with fictional euros; it does not recommend an amount, product or security.

## What the first visit should deliver

1. **Choose:** From the home page, enter one complete case without an account or personal questionnaire. Alex's first paycheque creates a familiar tension between accessible savings and a first investment.
2. **Understand:** See the cash consequence and trade-off of the selected action, with the other two actions available for comparison. Do not present one option as universally correct.
3. **Apply:** Answer one question that tests the transferable idea: when the money may be needed matters more than a market prediction. Show the reason for either answer and a question the learner can reuse.
4. **Continue:** Offer another choice, an optional four-question reflection, and the early access list for future cases. The list comes after a useful experience and does not gate the lesson.

## Three-level product direction

| Level | Learning goal | Example practical cases | Release state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Money foundations | Distinguish essentials, accessible savings and money available for longer goals. | A first paycheque; an unexpected expense. | First case available; broader level to design. |
| 2. First investments | Understand time horizon, risk, diversification and fees before choosing a product. | Start a modest routine; compare two diversified approaches. | Planned. |
| 3. Staying with a plan | Revisit decisions when circumstances or markets change. | A market fall; a new job or a shorter goal. | Planned. |

These are learning goals, not locked screens or promises of financial outcomes. Additional cases should be reviewed for factual accuracy and beginner comprehension before being offered.

The first case's educational framing follows the [AMF's guidance on organising savings](https://www.amf-france.org/fr/espace-epargnants/savoir-bien-investir/cadrer-son-projet/comment-organiser-son-epargne) and [matching a placement to its time horizon](https://www.amf-france.org/fr/espace-epargnants/savoir-bien-investir/cadrer-son-projet/fixer-son-horizon-de-placement). It intentionally avoids a universal cash target or a predicted market return.

## Design checks for the next iterations

- At 320 px, no horizontal scrolling; primary choices and actions remain readable and at least 44 px high.
- All choices, explanations, the checkpoint and the waitlist work with keyboard and visible focus. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Copy separates Alex's assumptions from the learner's answers. Amounts, cash consequences and the case preview agree across home, exercise and recap.
- An incorrect checkpoint answer teaches the principle and allows another attempt. Do not claim mastery from merely opening the recap.
- Validate the journey with 5–8 people from the target group: can they explain why accessible cash matters, find the next step and describe what Nudge offers without prompting?
