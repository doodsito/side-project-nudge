# Nudge — Product vision

## Product direction (established)

Nudge is a **mobile-first investing education app** for people who want to understand investing well enough to get started with confidence. Core learning happens through short explanations, realistic situations, and **practice decisions with virtual money**. The experience should feel accessible and practical without becoming a conventional online course website.

## Audience

Primary working audience: investing beginners, including university students and early-career professionals. Do not make student status a prerequisite to using Nudge.

## Experience principles

- Teach a concept, give the user a meaningful choice, explain the consequences, and guide their next step.
- Prefer practice and reflection to passive lesson consumption.
- Build trust through clear language, transparent simulations, and appropriately framed uncertainty.
- Make the first meaningful action easy to discover on mobile.

## Product boundaries

Nudge is **not** a brokerage, a personalized investment-advice service, or a generic budgeting tool. A simulated portfolio must be clearly presented as a learning environment, not as a prediction of real returns.

## Ideas under evaluation — not approved features

- A virtual €10,000 starting portfolio and outcomes that evolve with decisions.
- Market-linked events and data, ETF education/comparisons, recurring decisions.
- Points, progress maps, peers, and leaderboards to reinforce learning.

These are candidates for product testing; do not treat them as shipped or mandatory in future implementation tasks.

## Open questions

Final lesson format, progression mechanics, interaction frequency, and measurement of learning outcomes are still subject to product validation. Examine current implementation before documenting any journey as live.
