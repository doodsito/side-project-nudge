# Nudge — Brand identity

## Approved colour direction — Colour System C

**Violet / Gold / Teal:** warm, assured, contemporary. This is a confirmed decision, not an open palette exploration.

| Role      | Colour   | HEX       | Intended use                              |
| --------- | -------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Primary   | Violet   | `#6236E8` | Brand hierarchy and primary actions       |
| Secondary | Gold     | `#E6B44A` | Educational, practical, editorial content |
| Accent    | Teal     | `#2DC9BE` | Small highlights and progress details     |
| Canvas    | Lavender | `#F3F0FF` | Main light-mode background                |
| Card      | White    | `#FFFFFF` | Reading, forms, dialogs                   |
| Main text | Ink      | `#222039` | Body text on light surfaces               |

Apply **60 / 30 / 10** (Violet / Gold / Teal) as a guide in branded compositions, **not** a mandatory screen-wide percentage. Reading surfaces may be neutral.

Mascot pink belongs to character art and is **outside** the interface's three-colour palette. Gold must not be reused as the warning state; Teal must not be reused as the success state.

See `design-system.md` for complete scales and interaction rules. Visual reference: `references/Nudge_Colour_System_C_Only.pdf`.

## Brand expression — product design direction

- Friendly, encouraging, and credible; approachable without infantilizing the learner.
- Modern and practical rather than a generic trading dashboard.
- Focus attention on learning and one clear next step.

These describe product/UX direction, **not** a finalized typographic or illustration style guide.
